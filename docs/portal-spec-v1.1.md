# FONKON Market Development Portal v1.1

## Goal
Provide salespeople with a guided workflow that converts customer conversations into structured data and actionable opportunities.

## Navigation
- Today: tasks, overdue follow-ups, hot opportunities
- Customers: customer list, filters, score, owner
- New customer: identity + business facts
- Interview: guided questions mapped to data fields
- Opportunity: CV, OT, PI, AQ, stage, next action
- Dashboard: team score distribution, hot leads, pipeline

## Guided interview
Each question must display:
1. What to ask
2. Why it matters
3. Field code captured
4. Suggested next question based on the answer

## Score display
Show CV, customer grade, OT, PI and AQ only after required inputs are present. Show evidence completeness and data confidence separately.

## Warning states
- Red: low score or insufficient evidence; do not over-invest.
- Yellow: potential opportunity; complete missing evidence and validate.
- Green: priority opportunity; execute the next action.

## UX rule
The salesperson should never need to calculate a score manually.
